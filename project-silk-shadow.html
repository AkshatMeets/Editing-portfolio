Silk & Shadow

Client/Brand

Eles

Category

Editorial

Year

2026

Role

AI Generation, Editorial Direction, Retouching

Deliverables

Editorial Spread, Social Media Snippets

Silk & Shadow is an intimate editorial exploring the delicate interplay between fabric and light. Created for luxury intimates brand Eles, the series utilizes AI to generate soft, painterly scenes reminiscent of classical portraiture, but with a distinctly contemporary, editorial edge.

The post-production process involved rigorous retouching to perfect the translucency of lace and the gleam of satin against the skin. By stripping away extraneous background details and focusing purely on the subjects and their garments, we delivered a series that is both deeply emotive and exquisitely detailed.
