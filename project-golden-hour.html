Golden Hour

Client/Brand

Oasis

Category

Lookbook

Year

2026

Role

AI Generation, Resort Wear, Post-Production

Deliverables

Lookbook Catalog, IG Story Series

Radiant and leisurely, Golden Hour showcases Oasis' latest resort wear collection. Instead of flying a crew to a remote location, we leveraged AI to construct idyllic Mediterranean vistas bathed in perfect late-afternoon sunlight. The warm tones and long shadows provide a relaxed yet sophisticated backdrop for the flowing linen and silk pieces.

Post-production was crucial in refining the interaction of natural light and sheer fabrics. We enhanced the golden hour glow, ensuring the skin tones and textiles felt sun-kissed and vibrant, providing the brand with a cohesive and alluring visual narrative without the logistical complexities of an on-location shoot.
