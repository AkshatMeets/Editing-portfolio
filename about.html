About

AI fashion creative based in India.

I'm Zenitsu — an AI fashion creative specializing in AI-generated imagery and video for modern apparel brands.

Over the past 5 months, I've immersed myself in the intersection of artificial intelligence and fashion, developing hands-on expertise in creating commercial-ready fashion content using AI generation tools, creative direction, and professional post-production.

I work with clothing and apparel brands to transform their real garments into editorial-quality imagery and video — eliminating the need for traditional photoshoots while maintaining the visual standards the fashion industry demands.

My approach combines technical proficiency with AI tools with a deep appreciation for fashion aesthetics, brand identity, and commercial requirements. Every project starts with real garment references and ends with polished, deliverable assets.
