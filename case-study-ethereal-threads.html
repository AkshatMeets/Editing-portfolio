Ethereal Threads

Client: AETHON Category: AI Lookbook Timeline: 10 days Year: 2026

Complete AI lookbook creation for a luxury streetwear brand — from garment references to editorial-ready imagery.

Objective

AETHON, a rising luxury streetwear label, required a comprehensive 12-look AI lookbook for their upcoming SS26 collection. Seeking to innovate and bypass the logistical constraints of a traditional photoshoot, the brand wanted high-end editorial imagery that perfectly captured their avant-garde aesthetic while accurately representing their complex garment constructions.

Challenge

The primary challenge lay in maintaining strict garment accuracy and preserving the brand's unique DNA across diverse, AI-generated looks. Luxury streetwear often features intricate textures, specific draping, and proprietary hardware. Ensuring these elements remained consistent and photorealistic in a purely digital generation process required advanced prompt engineering and meticulous post-production.

Original Garment Reference

The process began with flat lays and basic fit photos provided by the AETHON design team.

Original garment references and texture swatches provided by the brand.

AI Output & Iteration

Initial generations focused on establishing the mood, lighting, and model casting. Through iterative prompting and ControlNet integration, we guided the AI to conform to the specific silhouettes of AETHON's designs.

Initial AI-generated outputs exploring mood and composition.

Editing & Correction Process

The raw AI outputs served as a sophisticated base. The critical final phase involved rigorous retouching: correcting structural anomalies, compositing accurate textures from the reference photos onto the generated garments, and applying a cohesive, cinematic color grade to unify the lookbook.

Detailed retouching, compositing, and color correction workflow.

Final Results

The resulting lookbook successfully merged high-fashion editorial aesthetics with precise product representation, delivering a compelling narrative for AETHON's SS26 collection.

Selected final imagery from the Ethereal Threads lookbook.

My Role

  • AI Creative Direction & Concept Development
  • Advanced Prompt Engineering & ControlNet Implementation
  • Image Generation & Curation
  • High-end Retouching & Compositing
  • Color Grading & Final Delivery Formatting

Deliverables

  • 48 final high-resolution images (4 per look)
  • 3 optimized social media edits
  • 1 comprehensive lookbook PDF layout
