Concrete Runway

Client/Brand

Vanguard

Category

Campaign

Year

2026

Role

AI Generation, Street Style, Creative Direction

Deliverables

Print Campaign, Digital Banners

Concrete Runway captures the raw, kinetic energy of global fashion capitals. This campaign for streetwear label Vanguard was designed to simulate candid, high-end street photography. By blending hyper-realistic subjects with slightly motion-blurred backgrounds, the AI generation accurately mimicked the fast-paced nature of fashion week documentation.

We directed the styling to feature layered, oversized silhouettes, bold graphics, and statement accessories. The final images underwent targeted color adjustments to evoke the gritty, cinematic feel of analog film stocks, elevating the brand's contemporary streetwear into the realm of high art.
