Ethereal Threads

Client/Brand

Aurum Studios

Category

Lookbook

Year

2026

Role

AI Creative, Generation, Retouching

Deliverables

24 Lookbook Images, Social Assets

The "Ethereal Threads" lookbook explores the intersection of high fashion and street sensibility, crafted entirely through advanced AI generation and meticulous post-production. Our approach centered around surreal, atmospheric environments that allowed the conceptual luxury garments to stand out, balancing stark architectural elements with soft, diffused lighting.

Through prompt engineering and localized editing, we maintained strict visual consistency across the entire collection. The garments feature flowing silhouettes and metallic accents that reflect a futuristic, yet deeply organic aesthetic. Each image was carefully retouched to ensure that textures, draping, and fine details matched the brand's premium positioning.
