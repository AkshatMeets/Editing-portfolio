Velvet Revolt

Client/Brand

Riot Studio

Category

Editorial

Year

2026

Role

AI Generation, Punk Fashion, Editing

Deliverables

Magazine Spread, Online Lookbook

Velvet Revolt channels the disruptive ethos of punk culture into a high-fashion framework. This editorial merges aggressive styling—heavy hardware, distressed leather, and clashing tartans—with refined, studio-quality portraiture. AI allowed us to experiment fearlessly with extreme hair and makeup concepts that would be arduous to achieve practically.

The editing process focused on maintaining a polished yet anarchic visual language. We implemented a high-contrast black-and-white treatment punctuated by vivid bursts of crimson and electric blue, creating an abrasive, unforgettable spread that challenges conventional beauty standards in editorial fashion.
