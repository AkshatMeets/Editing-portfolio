Urban Nomad

Client: NORTHBOUND CO. Category: AI Product Imagery Timeline: 3 weeks Year: 2026

AI product visuals for a modern outerwear brand — transforming flat lays into styled, commercial-ready imagery.

Objective

NORTHBOUND CO. needed comprehensive product imagery for their e-commerce platform and seasonal marketing materials. Seeking scalability and cost-efficiency without sacrificing premium aesthetics, they opted for an AI-driven approach over traditional studio product photography.

Challenge

The scale of the project was the primary hurdle. Ensuring consistent studio lighting, exact color accuracy matching the physical garments, and precise technical details (like zipper placement and seam sealing) across over 30 distinct product SKUs required an exceptionally structured workflow.

Original Garment Reference

Original garment references provided by the brand.

AI Output & Iteration

Initial AI-generated outputs focusing on fit and environment.

Editing & Correction Process

We developed a rigorous compositing pipeline. AI was used to generate perfect models and dynamic urban environments. The actual garments were then meticulously composited onto the generated models, followed by precise color matching and shadow reconstruction to ensure total commercial accuracy.

Compositing, color matching, and background integration process.

Final Results

Final e-commerce and lifestyle imagery.

  • Technical Color Matching
  • Final Retouching for E-commerce

Deliverables

  • 120+ e-commerce product images (4 angles per SKU)
  • 8 environmental lifestyle images
  • 1 animated brand video asset
