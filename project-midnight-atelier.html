Midnight Atelier

Client/Brand

Maison de Nuit

Category

Campaign

Year

2026

Role

AI Generation, Campaign Direction, Post-Production

Deliverables

Hero Campaign Visuals, Billboard Concepts

Midnight Atelier brings the nocturnal glamour of Parisian couture into a digital realm. Tasked with visualizing the brand's FW26 evening wear collection, we utilized sophisticated AI tools to render garments that shimmer and play with chiaroscuro lighting. The resulting campaign is moody, elegant, and intensely dramatic.

The post-production phase focused on emphasizing the deep blacks and jewel tones of the collection. Careful attention was paid to the simulated fabric textures—from crushed velvet to liquid silk—ensuring each piece looked undeniably luxurious and physically grounded within the surreal environment.
