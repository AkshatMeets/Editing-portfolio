Neon Dynasty

Client/Brand

Cybernetics Apparel

Category

Campaign

Year

2026

Role

AI Generation, AI Video, Futuristic Design

Deliverables

Digital Campaign, Motion Posters

Neon Dynasty pushes the boundaries of tech-wear by situating the collection in a striking, hyper-futuristic metropolis. This ambitious project integrated AI still generation with early-stage AI video techniques, creating dynamic motion posters that captured reflective fabrics reacting to neon environmental light.

The aesthetic combines cyberpunk grit with high-end editorial sheen. We carefully managed the lighting cues during generation to ensure vibrant, deeply saturated colors without succumbing to cheap, neon-soaked clichés. The final deliverables offer a sophisticated vision of future streetwear.
