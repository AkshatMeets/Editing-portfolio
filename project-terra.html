Terra

Client/Brand

Arbor

Category

Campaign

Year

2026

Role

AI Generation, Sustainable Fashion, Creative Direction

Deliverables

Digital Campaign, Ethical Lookbook

Terra is a visual ode to the natural world, crafted for sustainable fashion brand Arbor. By utilizing AI generation, the brand bypassed the significant carbon footprint associated with international location shooting. We crafted breathtaking, untouched landscapes—from rugged cliffsides to misty ancient forests—as the stage for their organic cotton and hemp collections.

The imagery highlights the raw textures and earthy dye palette of the garments. We employed subtle post-production techniques to simulate the organic grain of analog film, providing warmth and an authentic, grounding feel that resonates perfectly with the brand's eco-conscious messaging.
