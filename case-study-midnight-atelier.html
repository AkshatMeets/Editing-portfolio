Midnight Atelier

Client: MAISON NOIRE Category: AI Campaign Timeline: 2 weeks Year: 2026

AI-generated campaign imagery for a couture evening wear collection — concept, generation, and professional post-production.

Objective

MAISON NOIRE required a captivating digital campaign for their new couture evening wear line. The objective was to generate dramatic, high-end visuals featuring diverse AI models in luxurious, atmospheric settings, suitable for premium digital advertising and social media presence.

Challenge

Couture relies on the tactile reality of its fabrics. Achieving photorealistic rendering for delicate materials like translucent silk, layered tulle, and intricate beadwork via AI is notoriously difficult. The challenge was to prevent the garments from looking plastic or overly smooth, retaining the organic imperfections and luxurious drape of real haute couture.

Original garment references provided by the brand.

Editing & Correction Process

Significant post-production was dedicated to texture enhancement. We utilized frequency separation to introduce realistic fabric grains and meticulously dodged and burned highlights to emphasize the sheen of silk and the sparkle of crystal embroideries, bringing the digital fabrics to life.
